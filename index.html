Our story

Two benches. Forty years of hands.

Vesper & Thorne began when a goldsmith and a watchmaker shared a workshop and a stubborn idea: that the things you wear every day deserve the same care as heirlooms.

Every ring is set by hand. Every watch is assembled and regulated in-house. Every strap is cut, skived and stitched from leather we choose ourselves.
